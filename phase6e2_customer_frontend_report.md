## Manual smoke checklist

1. Signed out: `/admin/customers` redirects to sign-in. Signed in as a customer: "Admin access required".
2. Admin: "Customers" in the nav opens the list; search, status filter, paging and Clear update the URL.
3. Search with no match shows the empty state; a page past the end offers "Back to the first page".
4. Open a customer: profile, addresses, stats, recent orders (links open `/admin/orders/$orderId`).
5. A malformed id shows "Customer not found" without a request; an unknown valid id shows the same.
6. Suspend with a blank reason: blocked with "A reason is required." Suspend with a reason: badge
   flips after refresh; the audit log has `customer_suspended`. Reactivate likewise.
7. Try your own account (if reachable) and a staff account: refusal messages, status unchanged.
8. Click Confirm twice quickly: one request only; dialog cannot be closed while saving.
9. Save notes, reload: notes persist. Clear the box and save: "Notes cleared.", notes empty.
10. Confirm the visible note says suspension is a label only; place an order as a suspended customer
    and expect it to succeed (current behaviour).

## Decisions

- Suspension note: HANDOFF and the latest `create_order()` (20261003010000) show suspended customers
  are NOT blocked from ordering, so the status card and its dialog say so and never imply enforcement.
- Money: all figures are integer cents from the database, shown through `formatPrice`; the frontend
  adds nothing up. Stats exclude cancelled orders (database definition) and the page says so.
- After any status or notes call, queries are invalidated and the page shows the database's answer.
  The notes card remounts on the stored `notes_updated_at`, so the box shows what was kept.
- Recent orders have no order number in the RPC, so they show a short id reference (first 8 characters).
- `types.ts` gained the four RPC signatures (`Returns: unknown`; the parsers validate), the only
  edit outside customer files besides nav and the four static tests.
- Admin-only: since 6E.1B-3 the customer RPCs use `require_admin()`, so staff who are not admins get 403.
