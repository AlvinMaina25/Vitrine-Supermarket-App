## Manual smoke checklist

Admin

1. Sign in as admin; "Customers" appears in the nav and opens the list.
2. Search: type quickly, several characters, pause. Nothing typed is lost; the URL updates once. Try an emoji at the 100-character limit. Back/forward and Clear update the box.
3. Open a customer: profile, addresses, stats, recent orders.
4. Suspend with a blank reason: blocked. Suspend with a reason: badge flips; dialog has no "label only" text; the audit log has `customer_suspended`. Reactivate likewise.
5. Edit notes, save, reload: persisted. Clear and save: "Notes cleared." The card says "Only admins can see these."

Customer and staff denial 6. Signed out: `/admin/customers` goes to sign-in. Customer account: "Admin access required"; calling the four customer RPCs directly returns a denial and no data. 7. Staff (non-admin): the RPCs are denied; staff cannot read `customer_notes` or `admin_audit_log`.

Suspended customer 8. As a suspended customer, place an order: checkout shows "Your account can't place orders" with the fixed message, no raw database text, and no order is created. After reactivation the same basket orders normally. 9. A customer cannot set their own `profiles.status` (UPDATE grant excludes it; INSERT revoked).
