# Phase 16 manual QA (phone and tablet)

Test at 360 and 390 px (phone), 768 px (tablet), and rotate once. Real iPhone Safari and Android Chrome if you can.

1. Home, category, search: no sideways scroll; cards two across; sort and filter controls tappable; open the filter sheet.
2. Search box: tap it on iPhone; the page must not zoom; suggestions list fits above the keyboard.
3. Product page: long product name wraps; variant chips, stepper and sticky Add bar all tap cleanly; sticky bar clears the home indicator.
4. Basket: use a very long product name; stepper, Remove and Undo are easy to hit; totals unchanged.
5. Checkout: delivery and payment option cards are easy to tap; summary stacks under the form; M-Pesa phone field opens the number pad.
6. Sign in and account: email and phone fields use the right keyboard; no zoom on focus; address form selects and the default checkbox are easy to tap.
7. Admin nav: the strip scrolls sideways, every item is tappable; shell fills the screen with the browser bar showing and hidden.
8. Admin lists (orders, products, inventory, customers, payments, audit log): the table scrolls inside its card, the page itself does not; filter selects do not zoom.
9. Admin dialogs and forms (stock adjust, product edit, promotion create, delivery): dialog is never taller than the screen, scrolls inside, close button is easy to hit, Save is reachable with the keyboard open.
10. Keyboard only, then "reduce motion" on: focus ring is visible on every control above; no animation plays with reduce motion on.
